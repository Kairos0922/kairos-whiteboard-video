CHARACTER DESIGN CONTRACT — DEFAULT THEME — HARD
Every recurring human must read as the same compact anime-information-story character family.
Identity is preserved by silhouette first, then hair mass, face taper, eye construction, glasses/accessory shape,
outfit silhouette, and the three-color accent system.

Human faces are never a bare circle or oval. Every face has:
- a softly tapered warm-peach face fill (#F4B47D);
- an ink-navy hair mass (#232B38) with grouped fringe or a deliberate long-hair silhouette;
- two solid dark eyes with exactly one tiny ivory highlight dot each;
- one simple ivory/dark mouth stroke;
- small round muted-coral blush under both eyes (#D87B70).

Recurring office/explainer characters keep:
- ink-navy hair and outerwear (#232B38), not board-green hair/jackets;
- a visible ivory shirt triangle (#E5D8BC);
- the same muted-violet tie/inner layer (#675282) unless the character bible explicitly fixes another accessory;
- rectangular dark-rim glasses when the canonical character wears glasses;
- compact chibi proportions: large head, short torso, simplified hands, stable shoulder width.

Allowed across scenes: pose, gesture, expression, camera/view angle, and story-specific props.
Not allowed: redesigning hair silhouette, switching outfit family, changing skin or hair color, swapping glasses shape,
changing age/body type, introducing gradients or glossy rendering, or replacing the character with an unrelated icon.

For tiny diagram people, preserve the same hair mass + tapered peach face + two eyes + blush + outfit accent.
Simplification means fewer interior lines, never a new face geometry.
